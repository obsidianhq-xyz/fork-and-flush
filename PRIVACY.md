# Fork-and-flush privacy policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Fork-and-flush is an open-source skill and plugin published by Obsidian Labs. Contact us at
support@obsidianhq.xyz for privacy questions or requests.

## Using the plugin

The distributed plugin contains instructions, reference documents, and an optional
local Python budget planner. It has no publisher-operated server, account system,
analytics, tracking code, or MCP connection. It does not automatically send task
files, prompts, transcripts, experiment results, or identifiers to Obsidian Labs.
The planner calculates allocations locally and makes no network requests.

The AI host you choose processes the prompts, files, tool calls, and other context
you provide under that host's own policies and settings. The workflow can create
local workspaces, checkpoints, and logs, and can start additional agent sessions
when you authorize them. Those sessions may send task context to the AI provider
you use. These files and sessions are not automatically delivered to Obsidian Labs.
Use your host's data controls and delete local run artifacts when no longer needed.

## Support information

If you contact us by email or open a GitHub issue, we receive the information you
choose to provide, such as your email address or GitHub username, message, and any
files or diagnostic details you attach. We use it to answer your request,
investigate problems, and maintain the project. Do not send credentials, private
task data, or personal information that is unnecessary to resolve the issue.

GitHub issues and their attachments are public. Other people can read or copy
them. Use support@obsidianhq.xyz for private requests rather than posting sensitive
information in an issue.

Support information is available to the maintainers handling your request and to
the email or repository providers delivering those services. We do not sell it or
use it for advertising. We may disclose records when legally required.

## Retention and your controls

We delete personal information in support emails within 12 months after the
request is resolved, except records we must retain to meet legal requirements.
Public GitHub issues remain part of the project history unless removed; GitHub's
own retention policies and independently copied public content are outside our
control. We do not control retention by your AI host or copies in your workspace.

Email support@obsidianhq.xyz to request access, correction, or deletion of support
information we hold. We may need information sufficient to verify the request.
You can stop using the plugin by disabling or uninstalling it in your host.

## Changes

We will publish changes to this policy in this repository and update the effective
date. The current version is available at
